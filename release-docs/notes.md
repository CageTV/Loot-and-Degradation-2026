First release, a pre-release of the native rebuild of Loot and Degradation.

NEW
- One SKSE plugin replaces the original's Papyrus scripts: degradation of the player's tempered gear from hits and swings, per-material durability, optional breaking with scrap, followers (off by default).
- Tempered and enchanted NPC weapons and armor when NPCs load (the original's formulas and enchantment lists), remembered in the co-save.
- Equipment Repair Kit menu (improve weapons or armor away from a bench, damage equipment by a tier).
- Gear health widget through SKSE Menu Framework 3: percentage-based, alpha-based or both; original or new icons; icon colour; free position; layout presets.
- Settings pages in SKSE Menu Framework 3, saved to Data/SKSE/Plugins/LootandDegradation.ini.
- Installer with two DLLs: new (Skyrim 1.6.1170 and newer, 1.7.x included) and older (VR and 1.6.1130 and older, untested in game).
- Optional PBR textures for the whetstone and metal scrap (for PBR Nif Patcher or ParallaxGen / PGPatcher).
- Leftovers of the original that nothing uses any more (the Flash widget, the old menu translations and title image) are not included.
