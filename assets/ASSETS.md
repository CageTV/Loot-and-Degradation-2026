Assets of Loot and Degradation 2026, taken from the original Loot and Degradation 1.31 by isoku (CC BY-NC-SA); credits: richmondb (whetstone), psychosteve (widget icons).

Changes from the original files:
- textures/lootanddegradation/whetstone_n.dds: recompressed from uncompressed 32-bit to BC7 (350 KB -> 88 KB, same size and mip levels); the original is kept in ../assets-original.
- Everything else is byte-identical to the original: the two meshes (LE format NiTriShape, which Skyrim SE loads as it is), the break sounds (16-bit mono PCM WAV, valid for positional sound), the other textures, the widget swf and the translations.
